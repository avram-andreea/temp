First-timer guide

Everything you need before your first grip. session — arrival, kit, and how to choose Mega vs Studio.

01 Arrive

Come 15 minutes early for check-in and a short induction. You’ll meet the space, understand the machine or mat setup, and get settled before class starts.

02 Get grippy

Grip socks are included with introductory offers. Wear comfortable movement kit, bring water, and use lockers for bags and valuables.

03 Move your way

Every class includes modifications. Tell your instructor it’s your first time — beginner-friendly means supported, not soft.
