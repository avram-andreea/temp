What to expect

Every HIIT session lasts 50 minutes and is designed to challenge your entire body through carefully programmed intervals of work and recovery.

Classes combine functional movements, resistance equipment and bodyweight training to improve strength, fitness and overall athletic performance.

You’ll move with purpose, work at your own pace and be coached throughout by instructors who prioritise excellent technique just as much as intensity.

No two sessions are ever quite the same, but every class is built around one goal: helping you become stronger, fitter and more capable.

Before your first class

Please arrive 15 minutes before your first visit so your instructor can welcome you to the studio and answer any questions before class begins.

Please let your instructor know if you:

  • have any injuries
  • are pregnant
  • have recently had surgery
  • have any health concerns that may affect your session

Every movement can be modified to suit your experience and confidence.

What to bring

  • Water bottle
  • Comfortable, fitted activewear
  • A willingness to work hard

Is HIIT suitable for beginners?

Absolutely.

Every Grip HIIT session is designed with scalable options, allowing complete beginners and experienced members to train alongside one another.

Your instructor will demonstrate movement options, provide regressions where needed and encourage progress when you’re ready.

You don’t need to be the fastest or strongest person in the room.

You just need to show up.

Why choose HIIT?

HIIT at Grip isn’t about surviving fifty minutes of chaos.

It’s about training with purpose.

Every class is carefully programmed to develop strength, cardiovascular fitness and confidence through intelligent coaching, progressive overload and movements that transfer into everyday life.

You’ll leave feeling stronger, fitter and ready for whatever comes next, whether that’s another workout, a day at work or simply keeping up with life outside the studio.
