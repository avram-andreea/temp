Session type

Restorative Yin

Pause. Breathe. Let go.

Restorative Yin is our slowest and most restorative yoga practice, offering space to soften both body and mind through stillness, supported postures and mindful breathing.

In contrast to faster, more dynamic styles of movement, Yin invites you to stay with each posture for longer, allowing your muscles to relax while gently encouraging mobility through the body’s deeper connective tissues.

With carefully supported positions, calming guidance and an unhurried pace, this practice creates an opportunity to release tension, quiet the mind and reconnect with yourself.

There is nothing to achieve here.

Simply arrive exactly as you are.

Best for
Recovery · Mobility · Flexibility · Relaxation · Stress Relief

What to expect

Every Restorative Yin session lasts 50 minutes, followed by five minutes of guided mindfulness to leave you feeling grounded, balanced and ready for whatever comes next.

Using bolsters, blocks and supportive props where appropriate, you’ll move through a series of long-held postures designed to encourage deep relaxation while improving flexibility and joint mobility.

This is a quiet, nurturing practice where stillness becomes part of the work.

Expect slower breathing, softer movement and the rare opportunity to simply pause.

Before your first class

Please arrive 15 minutes before your first visit so your instructor can answer any questions before class begins.

Please let your instructor know if you:

  • have any injuries
  • are pregnant
  • have recently had surgery
  • have any health concerns that may affect your practice

Every posture can be modified to support your individual needs.

What to bring

  • Water bottle
  • Comfortable, warm clothing
  • Yoga mat (or use one of ours if provided)

Is Restorative Yin suitable for beginners?

Absolutely.

No previous yoga experience is required.

Your instructor will guide you through each posture, offering supportive modifications and encouraging you to listen to your body throughout the practice.

There is no competition, no expectation and no pressure to perform.

Only time to rest, restore and reconnect.

Why choose Restorative Yin?

Rest is an essential part of progress.

Whether you’ve spent the day at a desk, trained hard earlier in the week or simply need an hour away from constant demands, Restorative Yin provides the opportunity to slow down and reset both physically and mentally.

Sometimes the strongest thing you can do is stop.
