What to expect

Every Megaformer session lasts 50 minutes.

Every Grip Megaformer class finishes with five minutes of guided mindfulness.

Because leaving stronger is only half of it. We want you walking back into your day feeling calmer, clearer and more balanced than when you arrived.

Unlike traditional high-impact training, the challenge comes from time under tension rather than speed. Your instructor will coach your pace, spring selection and technique throughout the class, providing regressions and progressions so everyone can move confidently, regardless of experience.

Don’t be surprised if your muscles start shaking. That’s part of the process.

Before your first class

Please arrive 15 minutes before your first visit so your instructor can introduce you to the Megaformer, explain the springs and answer any questions before class begins.

Please let your instructor know if you have:

  • injuries
  • are pregnant
  • have recently had surgery
  • or have any health concerns that may affect your session.

What to bring

  • Grip socks (required for all Megaformer classes and available to purchase in studio)
  • Water bottle
  • Comfortable, fitted activewear

Is Megaformer suitable for beginners?

Absolutely.

Every Grip Megaformer class is programmed with multiple variations, allowing beginners and experienced clients to train together while working at completely different intensities.

Your instructor will coach you through spring choices, positioning and movement options throughout the class, so you’ll never be expected to keep up with anyone else.

The goal isn’t to be perfect.

The goal is simply to leave stronger than you arrived.
