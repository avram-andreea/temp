What to expect

Reiki at grip. is a quiet, restorative session - time set aside to slow down without needing to perform, push or keep up.

You’ll settle into a comfortable position and wrap up, while your practitioner places their hands lightly on or just above different areas of the body. You can close your eyes, let your breath settle naturally and simply be still.

If it’s your first time, your practitioner will guide you through what to expect so you can settle in comfortably.

There is nothing to achieve here. Just space to rest and unwind.

Before your first session

Please arrive 15 minutes before your first visit so we can welcome you to the studio and answer any questions before your session begins.

Please let your practitioner know if you:

  • have any injuries
  • are pregnant
  • have recently had surgery
  • have any health concerns that may affect your session

Your practitioner will talk you through what to expect so you can settle in comfortably.

What to bring

  • Comfortable, warm clothing
  • Water bottle
  • An open mind and a willingness to rest

Is Reiki suitable for beginners?

Absolutely.

No previous experience is required.

If it’s your first time, your practitioner will guide you through what to expect so you can settle in comfortably.

You don’t need to know what to do.

You just need to show up and rest.

Why choose Reiki?

Life asks us to move quickly.

Reiki gives you permission to stop.

It’s a gentle Japanese practice designed to help you slow down, soften and settle into stillness - whether you’ve trained hard earlier in the week, spent the day at a desk, or simply need an hour away from constant demands.

You’ll leave feeling calmer, clearer and more restored than when you arrived.
