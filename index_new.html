Your first class, without the guesswork.

  1. 01

    Arrive

    Come 15 minutes early for check-in and a quick induction so you know the room, the machine, and the flow.

  2. 02

    Get grippy

    Grip socks are included with intro offers. Bring water, wear movement-friendly kit, and leave valuables in the lockers.

  3. 03

    Move your way

    Every class includes low-impact options and modifications. Beginner-friendly doesn’t mean easy - it means supported.

  4. 04

    Stay for a little. Stay for a while

    Take time to reset before you continue with your day, rebalanced, refocussed, ready.
